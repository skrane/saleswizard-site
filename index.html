FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How does the AI coaching work?

SalesWizard records each sales conversation, scores it against the attributes that matter to your business, and highlights the key moments. It then shows your reps specific, better ways to respond, and learns from your top performers so the whole team is coached to the same standard.

How long does it take to get set up?

Setup is quick. We work with you to define the attributes you want to score, and your reps can start recording right from the mobile app the same day. There's no long onboarding project to work through.

Is it legal to record sales conversations?

In most places, yes. Some states require you to let the customer know the conversation is being recorded, so we give you simple guidance on notifying customers up front. We recommend confirming the rules for the states your team sells in.

How do reps feel about being recorded?

It's normal for reps to be hesitant at first. But once they see how quickly the coaching helps them improve, and close more, on the very next call, most come around fast. Top reps tend to embrace it as an edge over the competition.

How much does it cost?

SalesWizard costs $29–$49 per user per month, depending on your team size. Plans are billed month-to-month with no long-term contract.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. With SalesWizard, you pay month to month and don’t have to sign a long-term contract. This flexibility sets us apart from other platforms. Your team gets the sales structure and visibility it needs, without being tied down.
